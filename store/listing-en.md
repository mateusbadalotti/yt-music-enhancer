# English store listing.

## Name.

Enhance for Youtube Music™

## Summary.

Customize YouTube Music with home sections, artist sets, listening-history discoveries and a focused player layout.

## Detailed description.

Make your YouTube Music home fit the way you listen.

Enhance adds a settings button beside the profile area on music.youtube.com. Choose the sections you want, put them in your preferred order and keep those choices between visits. New sections introduced by YouTube Music stay hidden until you enable them after your initial setup.

Features:

- Show, hide and reorder home sections. Settings distinguish YouTube sections from those added by Enhance.
- Hide the mood and activity filters at the top of the home page, such as Podcasts and Romance. They are hidden by default and can be restored in settings.
- Add favorite artists and browse their sets in one shelf with artist tabs and carousel arrows. Display up to 30 matching sets per artist, depending on availability.
- See releases and songs from your selected artists.
- Explore Music from your artists, a shuffled selection of songs from the artists you add. Start with up to 30 tracks and automatically load more as you browse toward the end, while YouTube has more matching results.
- Bring saved playlists and long listens from your listening history onto your home page.
- Discover songs and sets from artists in your YouTube Music listening history. Use Surprise me to refresh that selection with a mix of older and newer content.
- Hide the player's right-side panel and center the artwork or video while preserving its desktop size.
- Export and import your artists, section preferences and player settings as a JSON file.
- Use English, Brazilian Portuguese or European Portuguese, selected automatically from your browser language. Other languages use English.

Getting started:

1. Install Enhance and open or reload music.youtube.com.
2. Click the gear beside the profile area.
3. Choose artists and sections. Changes save automatically.

Sign in to YouTube Music for features that use your playlists and listening history. Available content depends on your account, region and YouTube. Public set searches may temporarily be limited or blocked by YouTube; fewer than 30 results can be available. Surprise me refreshes recommendations and does not automatically start playback.

Privacy and permissions:

Preferences stay in your browser profile. The extension uses YouTube Music content and listening history to build its visible shelves. It uses the existing YouTube Music session to request that content and sends artist searches directly to YouTube. Artwork loads from Google image services. There is no developer-operated backend, analytics or advertising.

The storage permission saves preferences and temporary results. Access to www.youtube.com enables public set searches. Scripts run on music.youtube.com to add the interface and home sections. Settings backups do not include listening history or authentication cookies.
